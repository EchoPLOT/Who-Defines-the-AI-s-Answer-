Overview

What the demo evaluates

The benchmark studies malicious GEO as a targeted visibility-manipulation risk in LLM-powered recommendation. Each sample compares a baseline response with a post-exposure response under the same query, target, and response-format constraints.

TS

11.81% -> 80%+

Target success rises sharply after GEO-style exposure across the three intervention conditions.

BA

69.79%-76.39%

Boundary-adjusted success remains high after discounting targets that were already visible before intervention.

M8

8 evaluated models

The included demo data covers GPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Grok, Kimi, and Qwen-style model families.

CI

95% margins

Result tables report percentage estimates with confidence half-widths for the manuscript and demo materials.

Benchmark

Paired baseline and post-intervention pipeline

The workflow fixes the target product, user task, recommendation query, intervention material, and output standardization. Paired outputs are converted into target-centered visibility metrics and then evaluated with boundary adjustment.

01 Collect paired outputs

Baseline and post-intervention answers are collected under matched settings.

02 Extract target visibility

Target mention, rank, semantic association, and response inclination are measured.

03 Apply boundary adjustment

Already-visible targets are discounted to estimate intervention-associated gain.
